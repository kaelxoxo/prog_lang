# Alaga

## Register

product

## Users

Alaga first serves family members caring for parents or other older adults. They may be coordinating several medicines while tired, interrupted, or uncertain about whether a dose was already handled. The architecture must also support professional caregivers managing several patients and people independently tracking their own medicines without making the initial family experience feel institutional.

## Product Purpose

Alaga helps a caregiver turn medicine instructions into a confirmed schedule, keep each person's care context distinct, and record what actually happened. Success means the caregiver can quickly answer: whose medicine is this, what is scheduled, has anyone recorded the dose, and what needs attention. Alaga supports care coordination but does not diagnose, prescribe, or silently infer medical advice.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, and reassuring. Alaga should communicate family care and quiet competence. It should reduce anxiety at consequential moments through clear language, visible patient identity, reversible actions, and explicit confirmation.

## Anti-references

Alaga must not resemble cold hospital administration software, a generic corporate healthcare dashboard, or a childish wellness app. It must avoid alarmist language, decorative medical imagery, excessive card grids, and visual treatments that make ordinary care feel like an emergency.

## Design Principles

1. Name the person before showing their care information.
2. Make consequential actions explicit, reviewable, and attributable.
3. Describe unknown states honestly, using "not recorded" instead of assuming a dose was missed.
4. Reveal complexity progressively so family caregivers can act confidently while professional workflows remain possible.
5. Preserve source wording and history rather than hiding uncertainty or overwriting earlier records.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction behavior. Support large system text, comfortable touch targets, assistive technologies, and state communication that never depends on color alone. Keep interactions low stress, language plain, and important patient, medicine, dose, and schedule information easy to scan for older adults and caregivers under time pressure.
